## Project Overview
Onalenna Jewels is a handcrafted jewellery brand based in South Africa. This website serves as the brand's online presence, showcasing its jewellery collections, sharing its story, and allowing customers to submit custom jewellery enquiries and contact the brand.

## Website Goals
- Provide a professional and elegant online presence for the brand.
- Showcase jewellery products with images, descriptions, and prices in ZAR.
- Allow customers to request custom pieces via an enquiry form.
- Share the brand's story, mission, vision, and core values.

## Key Features
- **5 Pages:** Home, About Us, Our Collection, Custom Enquiry, Contact.
- **Responsive Navigation:** Consistent navigation menu across all pages.
- **Product Showcase:** 4 jewellery products with images, names, and prices (ZAR).
- **Comparison Table:** Product materials, prices, and stock availability.
- **Interactive Map:** Embedded Google Maps iframe on the Contact page.
- **Enquiry & Contact Forms:** Structured forms with labels, inputs, and validation.
- **Social Media Links:** Clickable hyperlinks to Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok.
