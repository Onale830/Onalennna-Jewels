Our Story

Onalenna Jewels was founded in 2020 from a passion for timeless beauty and genuine craftsmanship. Our founder, Onalenna, started crafting pieces in her small studio in Johannesburg, blending modern trends with traditional African artistry and heritage techniques.

What began as a personal dream has blossomed into a thriving jewellery house recognized for exceptional quality and unique designs. Every piece tells a story your story capturing moments of love, celebration, and milestones in your life.

Our Mission

To create exquisite, handcrafted jewellery that celebrates the beauty, elegance, and strength of every woman. Our mission is to make luxury accessible and personal, ensuring that every piece reflects individual style while maintaining the highest standards of quality and craftsmanship.

Our Vision

To become Africa's most trusted and beloved handcrafted jewellery brand, known for our commitment to excellence, innovation, and ethical practices. We envision a world where every woman owns at least one piece of Onalenna Jewels a treasured symbol of her uniqueness, confidence, and African pride.

Our Core Values

  • Craftsmanship: We are dedicated to creating jewellery with precision, passion, and pride in every detail.
  • Authenticity: Each piece is genuine, unique, and made with love never mass-produced or compromised.
  • Sustainability: We source ethically and use eco-friendly practices in our production process.
  • Accessibility: Luxury should be affordable. We offer premium quality without the premium price tag.
  • Community: We celebrate South African talent and support local artisans in our supply chain.

Meet Our Founder

Onalenna Sebela is a master jeweller with over 10 years of international experience. She trained under renowned Italian jewellers and has perfected her craft through years of dedication and innovation.

After completing her apprenticeship in Milan, Italy, she returned to her roots in South Africa with a vision to create world-class jewellery that honors African heritage while embracing contemporary design.

Her philosophy is simple: "Every piece of jewellery should tell a story and make the wearer feel extraordinary."

Why Handcrafted Jewellery?

In a world of mass production, handcrafted jewellery stands apart. Each Onalenna piece is individually created, meaning no two designs are exactly alike. We use traditional techniques combined with modern innovation to ensure that your jewellery is not just beautiful, but also durable and timeless.

Sustainability and Ethics

We are committed to responsible sourcing and ethical practices. All gemstones and metals are sourced from suppliers who maintain fair labor practices and environmental standards. We use recycled metals when possible and minimize waste in our production process.
