Our Story

Onalenna Jewels was founded in 2020 from a passion for timeless beauty and genuine craftsmanship. Our founder, Onalenna, started crafting pieces in her small studio in Johannesburg, blending modern trends with traditional African artistry and heritage techniques.

What began as a personal dream has blossomed into a thriving jewellery house recognised for exceptional quality and unique designs. Every piece tells a story your story capturing moments of love, celebration and milestones in your life.

Our Mission

To create exquisite, handcrafted jewellery that celebrates the beauty, elegance and strength of every woman. We make luxury accessible and personal, ensuring that every piece reflects individual style while maintaining the highest standards of quality and craftsmanship.

Our Vision

To become Africa's most trusted and beloved handcrafted jewellery brand, known for our commitment to excellence, innovation and ethical practices. We envision a world where every woman owns at least one piece of Onalenna Jewels a treasured symbol of her uniqueness, confidence and African pride.

Our Core Values

  • Craftsmanship: We create jewellery with precision, passion and pride in every detail.
  • Authenticity: Each piece is genuine, unique and made with love.
  • Sustainability: We source ethically and use eco-friendly practices.
  • Accessibility: Premium quality without the premium price tag.
  • Community: We celebrate South African talent and support local artisans.

Meet Our Founder

Onalenna Sebela is a master jeweller with over 10 years of international experience. She trained under renowned Italian jewellers and has perfected her craft through years of dedication and innovation.

After completing her apprenticeship in Milan, Italy, she returned to her roots in South Africa with a vision to create world-class jewellery that honours African heritage while embracing contemporary design.

Her philosophy is simple: "Every piece of jewellery should tell a story and make the wearer feel extraordinary."

Why Handcrafted Jewellery?

In a world of mass production, handcrafted jewellery stands apart. Each Onalenna piece is individually created, meaning no two designs are exactly alike. We combine traditional techniques with modern innovation so your jewellery is beautiful, durable and timeless.

Sustainability and Ethics

We are committed to responsible sourcing and ethical practices. All gemstones and metals are sourced from suppliers who maintain fair labour practices and environmental standards. We use recycled metals when possible and minimise waste in our production process.
